# Content Questions

Ask these before replacing the placeholders in the website.

## General

- [ ] What name should the website or service use?
- [ ] What instructor name should be shown?
- [ ] What short introduction does the instructor want?
- [ ] What tone should the site use: calm, friendly, professional, supportive, clinical, or community-focused?
- [ ] Should the site mention only hypopressives, or should any other services be mentioned later?

## Hypopressive Training

- [ ] How does the instructor describe hypopressives in her own words?
- [ ] Does she want to use the current wording about core, pelvic floor, posture, and breathing?
- [ ] Does she want to include the history of hypopressives and Dr Marcel Caufriez?
- [ ] What benefits does she want to explain publicly?
- [ ] Does she have approved wording for pelvic floor function, postnatal recovery, back pain, or respiratory issues?
- [ ] Who does she normally help with hypopressive training?
- [ ] Are sessions 1-to-1, group sessions, online, in person, or a mixture?
- [ ] Where are sessions held?
- [ ] What days and times are available?
- [ ] How much do sessions cost?
- [ ] Does she offer a consultation or first session?
- [ ] What should people wear or bring?
- [ ] Does she have qualifications or certifications she wants listed?

## Safety And Suitability

- [ ] Are there any people who should not start without checking with a health professional first?
- [ ] What safety wording does she want included?
- [ ] Does she want a stronger disclaimer about medical advice, physiotherapy, or treatment?
- [ ] How should people discuss private health concerns before booking?
- [ ] Does she want to ask people to complete any screening before a session?

## Contact

- [ ] What phone number should be used?
- [ ] Should phone calls be allowed, or should enquiries be WhatsApp only?
- [ ] What WhatsApp Business number should be used?
- [ ] Does she want an email address shown?
- [ ] What default WhatsApp message should open when people click the button?
- [ ] What booking process should the website describe?

## Photos And Trust

- [ ] Does she want a photo of herself on the website?
- [ ] Does she have approved session photos?
- [ ] Does she have permission from people in any photos?
- [ ] Does she have testimonials?
- [ ] Does she have permission to publish testimonials?
- [ ] Does she want to show qualifications, certificates, or experience?

## Practical Details

- [ ] Any parking details?
- [ ] Any accessibility or venue access details?
- [ ] Any location notes, such as entrance, room name, or nearby landmarks?
- [ ] Any age restrictions or suitability notes?
- [ ] Any cancellation policy?
- [ ] Any payment methods?
