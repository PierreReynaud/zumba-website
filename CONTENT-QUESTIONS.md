# Content Questions

Ask these before replacing the placeholders in the website.

## Class Basics

- [ ] What name should the class or website use?
- [ ] What is her instructor name?
- [ ] What short introduction does she want?
- [ ] Where are the classes held?
- [ ] What day and time are the classes?
- [ ] How much does each class cost?
- [ ] Does she offer a first class free, trial session, or introductory price?
- [ ] Does she want people to book first?

## Contact Details

- [ ] What WhatsApp Business number should be used?
- [ ] Should phone calls be allowed, or WhatsApp only?
- [ ] Does she want an email address shown?
- [ ] Should the WhatsApp group only be shared manually?

## Photos And Tone

- [ ] Does she have class photos?
- [ ] Does she have permission from people in any photos?
- [ ] Does she want a photo of herself on the website?
- [ ] What tone does she want: energetic, calm, friendly, professional, community-focused?

## Practical Details

- [ ] Any health or safety wording she wants included?
- [ ] Any parking details?
- [ ] Any accessibility or venue access details?
- [ ] Any location notes, such as entrance, room name, or nearby landmarks?
- [ ] Any age restrictions or suitability notes?
- [ ] What should people wear?
- [ ] What should people bring?
- [ ] What payment methods are accepted?
- [ ] Should people arrive early for their first class?
